Nature on the Move with Miss Becky

I am thrilled to be continuing the Nature on the Move program that has been coming to WUNS for 18 years. I believe that one way to preserve our natural resources is to teach the youngest members of our society to love and respect the natural world around them. Nature on the Move allows children to get up close to Missouri’s wildlife and learn how each living thing affects the world in which they live. I will visit WUNS once per month with a lively presentation on a variety of topics.

October 14: Scary Animals
