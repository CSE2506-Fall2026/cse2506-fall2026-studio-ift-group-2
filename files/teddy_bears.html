Teddy Bears (Ages 2½–3)

Our classes focus on social development and learning through play. Each year’s class builds on the previous year’s program in curriculum experiences and activities offered. The classrooms are set up to invite exploration and foster a lifelong love of learning. Our staff is deeply committed to the young child and the importance of early childhood education.

About the Teddy Bear Classroom

Twos/Young Threes: Children must turn 3 by February 1 of that school year. The focus of this class is on social development and learning through play. Separating from parents, learning to take direction, and being nurtured by people outside of immediate family are important milestones for this age. The building of trust and growing peer awareness are enhanced by a safe environment that provides a foundation for learning.

The Teddy Bear class is the morning session for 2 1/2 – and 3-year-old children. Teachers design a variety of engaging activities that gradually encourage children to interact and communicate with others. The indoor classroom and outdoor playground are safe, interesting spaces for Teddy Bears to explore and make sense of their world.

Classroom Recaps

December – Winter and Holidays

The Teddy Bears learned about hibernation and animals that sleep during the winter, including groundhogs, bats, frogs, and snakes. We also talked about family traditions, learned about Hanukkah, made holiday artwork, and baked bread.

November – Colors and Fall

We explored primary colors through painting, matching, and mixing colors. The children also learned about fall leaves by counting, comparing sizes, and making leaf prints.

October – Pumpkins and Nature

The Teddy Bears explored pumpkins, learned about their life cycle, and practiced fire safety. We also talked about animals such as bats, spiders, owls, and snakes.

September – Classroom Routines

The children spent time getting to know one another and learning classroom routines. We explored art, music, Spanish, outdoor play, and bear-themed activities.

April – Spring

We learned about spring through painting, observing chicks and caterpillars, and exploring plants and flowers on the playground.

More information about our classroom activities can be found in the Teddy Bear newsletters.

Classroom Goals

The nursery school ensures that our school environment is safe, healthy and conducive to children’s exploration and independence. Our teachers encourage students to:

  • Explore, try and expand their experiences and interests.
  • Express themselves through language, drama, art, music and play.
  • Use their words to solve problems.
  • Recognize the rights, feelings and property of others.
  • Develop their motor coordination.
  • Increase their attention span.
  • Become more independent.
  • Appreciate language, creativity, literacy and numbers in many different forms.
  • Recognize themselves as competent, interested learners.
